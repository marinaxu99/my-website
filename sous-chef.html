A kitchen companion

Web Sous-Chef is an interactive website I designed for Design & Interactive Experiences. It brings together a recipe generator, a mystery-recipe discovery flow, and small kitchen tools.

The robot, playful lettering, sounds, and little animations give practical tasks a lighter personality.

Small interactions, useful outcomes

The toolkit includes a cooking timer, temperature conversion, and volume conversion. The recipe flow starts with the ingredients you have; the mystery box introduces a little surprise through recipe videos.

I built the interface with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, exploring API integration alongside the visual and interaction design.

Still evolving

The AI recipe connection needs another pass. I’m keeping this here as a record of the project, including the parts I want to improve.

The live site is an experimental demo. AI recipe generation may be unavailable.
