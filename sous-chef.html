A kitchen companion

Web Sous-Chef is an interactive project designed for Design & Interactive Experiences. It explores how a digital tool can quietly support the rhythm of everyday cooking, bringing together a recipe generator, a discovery flow, and core stovetop utilities into a unified space.

An illustrated robot mascot, playful typography, and subtle micro-interactions bring lighthearted personality to purely practical kitchen tasks.

Small interactions, useful outcomes

The interface integrates a rolling kitchen timer, dual-unit temperature conversion, and a fraction-friendly volume calculator into a persistent dock. Instead of prescriptive steps, the recipe flow starts with what is already in your pantry, offering immediate directions through lightweight client-side interactions.

Built with semantic HTML, modern CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, balancing API integration with intuitive visual design.

Still evolving

The AI integration remains an active experiment. I keep this case study visible as an honest archive of the design process—capturing both what works and the constraints I am continuing to refine.

The live site is an experimental prototype. AI recipe generation may be temporarily unavailable.
