A little about me

Hi, I’m Marina.

I design, make films, build things for the web, and sometimes throw clay.

I like moving between different ways of making. A website gives me a way to organize an idea. A film brings people together around a story. Clay asks me to slow down and try again.

At Arki Pictures, I work across visual design and production. Alongside that, I explore digital experiences and small tools, and I’m building a ceramics practice one piece at a time.

This website is my personal archive: a place to gather what I’ve made, what I’m learning, and the projects I want to keep working on.
