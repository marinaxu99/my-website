Beginning with the material

NAN is the name I’m giving to an evolving ceramics practice. For now, I’m learning and making: working with clay, repeating forms, and paying attention to what changes from one attempt to the next.

I’m drawn to functional objects and the small signs of the hand that remain in them.

A slower kind of making

Alongside websites and film, clay gives me a different pace. The process is physical, and the result doesn’t always follow the plan. That’s part of why I want to keep doing it.

A personal practice in progress. There is no collection for sale at the moment.
