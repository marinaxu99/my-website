An everyday admin problem

Wrap Sheet is an invoice-making website I’m working on. The idea is to reduce the copying and organizing between a receipt and an invoice.

I’m exploring AI-assisted receipt scanning and phone-camera OCR, so receipt details can be reviewed and added to an invoice.

The part I’m exploring

How can a tool make the repetitive work easier while keeping the details easy to check? The flow I’m working toward is simple: collect a receipt, review the information, and bring it into the invoice.

An active work in progress. The cover here is a project title graphic, not a screenshot of the current interface.
